Set up your Instagram grid preview

Four short steps. When you're done you'll have a link to paste into Notion, and your planned posts will show up as an Instagram grid right on your calendar page.

1Connect Notion

…Checking your Notion key…

2Pick your content calendar

First give the widget access to your calendar:

  1. Open your content calendar database in Notion (as a full page).
  2. Click ••• in the top-right corner → Connections → choose the connection you made in step 1.
  3. If your calendar links to a Clients database, share that one too so the account picker works.
  4. Click Share → Copy link, and paste the link below.

3Make it yours

Changes show up in the preview straight away.

What the dot colors mean
  • Idea / drafting any other status
  • Ready for review "review", "pending"
  • Changes requested "changes", "revise"
  • Approved "approved", or an Approved checkbox
  • Scheduled "scheduled", "queued"
  • Posted "posted", "published", "done"

4Add it to Notion

  1. On your Notion page, type /embed and press Enter.
  2. Paste the link and click Embed link.
  3. Drag the edges of the embed to about phone width (around 500 px wide, 800 px tall).

Your grid updates by itself. To add a photo, upload it to the post's Cover Image property in Notion, then press Refresh on the grid.
